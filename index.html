About Professor Solaiman

Professor Abul Hasnat Muhammad Solaiman, PhD (Japan) is a distinguished academician, senior researcher, and technology pioneer in agricultural development. Serving as a senior Professor in the Department of Horticulture at Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University (SAU), his career is defined by driving high-impact technology adoption and establishing advanced research ecosystems within national agricultural frameworks.

He is prominently recognized as the Founder and Former Project Director of FAB LAB SAU, an advanced fabrication and rapid prototyping facility funded by the World Bank Group that bridging industrial maker cultures with precision agriculture.

Strategic Mandates & Commercial R&D

Beyond academic pedagogy, Dr. Solaiman orchestrates vast agricultural projects linking academic innovations directly to commercial value chains:

  • Market Intelligence Architectures: Directs multi-tenant student analytics cohorts evaluating vegetable market price volatility distributions dynamically across wholesale distribution centers.
  • Agricultural Infrastructure Optimization: Orchestrates operational layout strategies for key distribution centers, optimizing efficiency vectors while introducing clean tech, green initiatives, and strict hygiene protocols.
  • Agro-Export & Plantation Projects: Authoring comprehensive business and logistics blueprints for macro-scale fruit plantation installations in key agricultural zones like Sylhet, alongside export framework structuring.
  • Contract Farmer Scaling: Designing and delivering high-yield agricultural training toolkits, expanding modern agronomy procedures to regional farming groups.
